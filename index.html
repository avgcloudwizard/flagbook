How to play & keyboard controls

Find the named country by clicking its territory. A wrong guess stays in the same round; hints and reveals count as assisted rounds. A clean round means finding it on your first click without a hint. Continent filters change the countries you are asked about; the whole map stays visible for context.

Focus the globe, use arrow keys to move a country under the centre crosshair, and press Enter to choose it. Use + and − to zoom. Small dots make tiny states clickable. Changing continent starts a new round.

Attempts are saved in this browser and included in your progress backup.

Higher or lower?

Fair play & how the scores work

Each game keeps its own completed-round count. A round is clean when every answer is correct and you use no extra clues or reveals. Duplicate top-five answers do not count as mistakes. Changing game, subject or topic starts a new round.

Comparisons use reported figures from one period, or a geographic reference. Equal values are not used in Higher or Lower. Top Five includes countries tied at the cutoff, and ranks cover this catalogue’s reporting countries. Mixed-date gold holdings and shared natural features are excluded from country comparisons. “Surprise me” starts with familiar topics; use the topic list to explore the full range.

Attempts are saved in this browser and included in your progress backup.
