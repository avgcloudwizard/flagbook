# Unexpected Connections: research batch B

Verified 23 October 2026. Nine new stories in `connections-b.json`, each with a 100–109-word explanation and directly supporting sources read during research. No production-rank cards are included.

## Existing-content review

Reviewed the existing `data/fun-facts.json` claims. None of these nine claims already exists. Chilli and potato appear in commodity production cards, but those are different underlying claims from the journeys, botanical distinctions and adaptation described here. The old geographical descriptions of Spain/Portugal/Cape Verde mention Atlantic archipelagos, not Isleño settlement or the ukulele connection.

## Sources and qualification decisions

- Isleños: National Park Service page, updated 21 March 2026, directly supports the 1778 arrival, Spanish colonial context, soldier-settlers and later wetland livelihoods. Avoided claims about every resident of Louisiana and avoided exact language-survival estimates.
- Montserrat: Tourism Authority festival and history pages directly support the public holiday, African/Irish heritage, thwarted 1768 uprising and British Overseas Territory status. **Did not repeat the source’s “only country outside Ireland” wording**, which conflates political status and invites unsupported exclusivity.
- Geet-Gawai: the requested UNESCO ICH page challenged automated access, and Mauritius National Heritage Fund PDF endpoints intermittently redirected to maintenance. Used the accessible UNESCO Multimedia Archives description, UNESCO’s Aapravasi Ghat history, and a firsthand article by Bhojpuri Speaking Union leader Dr Sarita Boodhoo instead. The latter directly links the songs to Indian indentured migrants and describes intergenerational transmission. No population share from the nomination was used.
- Chilli: Kew’s full Capsicum annuum species profile supports American origins, pre-conquest cultivation, seeds to Europe, subsequent Portuguese-colonial cultivation in India, and the botanical distinction from black pepper. Did not invent an introduction date or a specific ship/voyage.
- Kochi: read Government of Kerala’s full synagogue description and NCBS/TIFR’s archival tile description. Together these support eighteenth-century Canton tiles, trader Ezekiel Rahabi, nineteenth-century Belgian chandeliers and the 1568 building date. **Omitted conflicting copper-plate dates** and unsupported “oldest” superlatives.
- Ukulele: University of Hawaiʻi’s article directly supports Portuguese woodworkers in 1879, Hawaiian adaptation and Kalākaua’s promotion. Hawaii News Now’s original interview with historian Jim Tranquada supports the Madeira connection. Smithsonian pages returned server errors on opening, so those are not retained as published evidence. **Omitted competing naming legends**, exact first-workshop dates and names that differed between sources.
- Breadfruit: Kew’s complete Artocarpus altilis profile supports both Bligh expeditions, the 1789 mutiny and the stated purpose of feeding enslaved plantation workers. No precise sea route or claim that the failed Bounty expedition delivered the crop is included.
- Potatoes: Max Planck’s report of the 2019 historical-genome study supports the short-day adaptation problem and later Chilean ancestry; Europeana supports Indigenous Andean domestication and the colonial movement to Spain. Avoided assigning one fixed ancestry to all modern varieties.
- Maltese: National Council for the Maltese Language supports Arabic roots, later independent development, Latin script, and Sicilian/Italian/English influences. Omitted its dated population figures and **did not claim Maltese is merely Arabic written differently**.

## Extra candidate researched, not selected

Tomatoes’ American roots and Italian association: read Kew’s *Tasty tomatoes* (25 October 2019), and examined primary research/reference leads on introduction and domestication. Held for a later batch to avoid too many variations of the same American-crop-to-Europe story; the potato story offers the additional mechanism of day-length adaptation. Exact tomato domestication location and individual introduction routes need careful qualification. This is an editorial exclusion, not a claim that the general connection is false.

## Maps and integration

All points are deliberately approximate; every connection is marked schematic, not a reconstructed voyage. Kochi’s map shows the China–India tile connection; the separate Belgian connection is explicit in the caption and prose. Malta’s two points show one language-contact connection, not the whole linguistic family tree. Use the text’s precise political names: Montserrat is a British Overseas Territory; Tahiti is in French Polynesia. Country filter codes `gb` and `fr` are modern browsing associations, not historical sovereignty assertions. Hawaiʻi is tagged `us` for modern browsing while the accuracy note states that it was a kingdom in 1879.

Related IDs all refer to other records in this batch; they can be cross-linked to other batches during merging. Fact JSON contains no live-generation dependency.
